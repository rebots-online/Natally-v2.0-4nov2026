I have designed the **Mobile Top Up** screen tailored strictly to the Natally brand language and exact specifications:

### Design Highlights & Brand Continuity
- **Palette**: Very dark plum-black `#120c1c` base, deep purple panels (`#1a1226` default, `#2a1d3d` selected option), `#3a2d50` borders, `#f1e8d8` warm cream text, and `#c9a24a` / `#f2ca50` antique gold accents.
- **Typography Hierarchy**:
  - **Fraunces**: Header ("Top Up"), section title ("What credits do"), and pricing tiers.
  - **Cinzel**: Small-caps labels ("CHOOSE AN AMOUNT", "CLOSE", and the primary CTA).
  - **Nunito Sans**: Clean, legible body copy for bullet points and checkout terms.
- **Components & Layout**:
  - **What credits do**: Informational panel with custom gold bullets containing verbatim copy.
  - **Choose an amount**: Three large selectable rows (`US$5 — 200 Cr`, `US$10 — 400 Cr` with gold border & active radio indicator, `US$25 — 1,000 Cr`).
  - **Note & Button**: Informational legal copy and a prominent 52px touch-target CTA: `Continue to payment · US$10`.
  - **Fidelity Guarantee**: Zero gradient washes, zero emojis, strictly verbatim copy without extra badges or features.
