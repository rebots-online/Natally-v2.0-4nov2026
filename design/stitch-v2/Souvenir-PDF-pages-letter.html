DEEP DIVE FOLIO III

The setting place

Origins

Known classically as Dysis, the Western threshold where planets descend beneath the horizon, marking the transition from self toward the mirror of another.

Traditional interpretation

Governs one-to-one covenants, spouses, sworn pacts, legal adversaries, and all formal treaties entered with external sovereign minds.

Lore

Ancient grimoires deemed this place the crucible where private resolve is tempered by mutual duty under the evening star.

Examples

A planet positioned here reflects how relationships test personal values and draw out qualities that cannot mature in solitude.

Astrology is an interpretive tradition, not a prediction.
