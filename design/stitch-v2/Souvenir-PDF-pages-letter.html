Origins

Known classically as Dysis, the Western threshold where planets descend beneath the horizon, anchored by the ☍ Descendant directly polar to the ASC Ascendant of the 1st House, marking the transition from self toward the mirror of another.

Traditional interpretation

Governs one-to-one covenants, spouses, sworn pacts, legal adversaries, and all formal treaties entered with external sovereign minds. Archetypally guided by ♎ Libra with natural ruler ♀ Venus and elevated under the sobering gaze of ♄ Saturn.

Lore

Ancient grimoires deemed this place the crucible where private resolve is tempered by mutual duty under the evening star, balancing desire with sacred contracts.

Examples

A planet positioned here reflects how relationships test personal values and draw out qualities that cannot mature in solitude.
