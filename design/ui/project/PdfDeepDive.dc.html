HOUSES · VII · IN TRADITION

The setting place

ok wait, the seventh house is literally where your people walk in. I love this one so much

Origins

The seventh house begins at the Descendant, the point on the western horizon where the Sun and planets set. Hellenistic astrologers named this angle the setting place (dysis) and counted it among the four angular, most powerful positions in a chart, opposite the rising Ascendant.

Traditional interpretation

Because it faces the first house of the self, tradition gives it the people who stand opposite you: marriage partners, partners in business, those you make agreements with, and also open adversaries. William Lilly's Christian Astrology (1647) lists marriage, contracts, lawsuits and open enemies under this house.

Lore

Twentieth-century astrology paired each house with a sign in order, so the seventh is often linked with Libra and its ruler Venus, the sign of balance and of the scales held between two sides.

Examples

  • Venus here is traditionally read as favourable for partnership.
  • Saturn here is traditionally read as commitment that comes slowly, or with an older or more serious partner.
  • An empty seventh house is common; its ruler, wherever it sits, then describes partnership instead.
